Beach Kids Program Guide

Everything you need to run a safe, fun Beach Kids week. Ages 6–7. Goal: get kids excited about the water and wanting to come back.

🏖️ Program Overview ▼
⭐
The goal is fun. After safety, fun is the most important thing. Older and more mature kids can be encouraged to steer or control sails — but the priority is getting every child comfortable around the water and excited to come back.

👶 Age group

6–7 year olds. Often their first experience on the water. Some will never have been near a boat.

📅 Format

5-day camp. Half day. Each day has a theme — canoeing, Opti rides, beach day, mini sail, Hobie day.

👥 Ratios

1 instructor + 1 volunteer minimum. Coach boats required for all on-water activities. Never more than 2 kids per boat with an adult.

🔧 Pre-Class Setup (Every Day) ▼

👤 Lead Instructor

  • Prep all boats and equipment before class time — coordinate with other classes, use whatever they don't need as a crucial component of their sessions
  • Be in the classroom at least 15 minutes before class starts
  • Have a more mature volunteer in the room as backup — needed if a child has to leave the group for a washroom break
  • On Day 1: be present at the entry gate or classroom to meet parents. Answer questions — this may be their child's first camp.
  • Confirm with parents: water bottle, sunscreen, beach clothing, hat, sunglasses
  • Entertain early arrivals with colouring or games (wax museum, playlist game)

⛵ Support Instructor

  • On Day 1: bring kids from the entry gate to the classroom
  • Help set up classroom — chairs, materials for the day's activity
  • Get coach boat ready for on-water sessions
  • Confirm boat/equipment availability with other classes
⚠️
Kids are never left alone — ever. If a child needs to go to the washroom or leave the group, an instructor or volunteer goes with them. One person must always remain with the rest of the group.
📅 Day-by-Day Schedule ▼

Monday — Canoeing Day

Get kids on the water from Day 1. Build excitement for the rest of the week.

Pre-class: confirm enough canoes for Beach Kids AND canoeing classes

9:00
Inside games — anyone can join as they arrive (last kid usually walks in around 9:20). Heads Up 7 Up, Animal Charades, Would You Rather
9:30
Name game — normal icebreaker or storytelling chain (say your name then continue a story)
9:45
Canoe lesson — diagram (which way is forwards, where to sit), how to paddle (one hand on top, other gripping side thumb up, push water backwards, pull paddle all the way out on return), pass paddles around to try, form groups of 3–4, practice paddling motion in classroom
10:00
Wetsuits & life jackets — tighten properly. Try to remember wetsuit sizes — saves a lot of time on following days
10:15
Launch — go against the current on the first half. If pushed west, go to an eastern beach and vice versa.
10:30
Games & snacks on the beach — Octopus, Limbo (bring a rope)
11:00
Head back — takes longer on the way back, current will help push you
11:15–11:30
Land — get kids to help pull canoes up, then remove wetsuits and life jackets before the area gets crowded
11:45
Back to classroom — award kids who did well. Build excitement for Opti rides tomorrow.

Tuesday — Opti Rides

First time on a sailboat. Keep it magical — this might be the moment they fall in love with sailing.

Pre-class: confirm enough Optis for other classes AND Beach Kids

9:00
Inside games — same rotation as Monday
9:45
Boat parts lesson — show diagram: Sail, Mast, Boom, Daggerboard, Sheet. They don't need tiller/rudder today. Kids draw the boat and compete for who includes the most parts. Explain sail trim: "A flappy sail is NOT a happy sail. When in doubt, let it out."
10:00
Wetsuits & life jackets
10:15
Show a real Opti on shore — get kids to name the parts, let them sit in it on the beach
10:30
First half goes sailing — 1 coach/volunteer + 1–2 kids per boat. Stay up-current. Stay close to shore. Second half on beach: snacks, beach maze, dig a big hole.
11:00
Switch — second half goes sailing
11:30
Pull up boats — kids help. Remove wetsuits and life jackets.
11:45
Back to classroom — award kids who did well on drawing, naming parts, and sailing

Wednesday — Beach Day

No water today. Build teamwork, familiarity with the environment, and a sense of accomplishment.

9:00–9:30
Icebreakers — Name + favourite sea animal, Zip Zap Zop, Handshake Murder, Musical Chairs, Freeze Dance
9:30–10:30
Beach art — kids collect sand, shells, sticks, seaweed on the beach, bring back to classroom and make art (stick to canvas, trace, paint). While this happens one instructor sets up the scavenger hunt.
10:30–10:45
Snack break
10:45–11:15
Scavenger hunt — clue-based or checklist version (see Activities section). Kids can split into teams or work together.
11:15–11:45
Pirate game — rig a Zest or Escape on land. Kids take turns being "pirates." Instructors splash water on them, make up stories, show them how to sail. Goal: make them excited about being on the water.
11:45–12:00
Pack up and head home

Thursday — Mini Sail Day

Steering practice with an instructor in the boat. Tacking with assistance. Certificates for first-time sailing.

9:00–9:30
Inside games — Night at the Museum (usually a favourite), Splat, Hangman, Pictionary, Musical Chairs
9:30–9:45
Booklet pages — parts of a boat, colour in boat parts, tacking dance practice
9:45–10:00
Prep — bathroom break, life jackets
10:00–10:15
Land demo — go over tacking and parts of boat on a real boat on shore. Make students point out the parts. Launch.
10:15–11:40
Sailing in groups of 2 — 1 instructor or volunteer per boat. Instructor starts steering, gradually hands over as kids get comfortable. Typically 2 Optis on water at a time, about 20 minutes per session — kids take multiple turns. Beach: soccer, sandcastles.
10:45–11:00
Snack break on beach
11:40–12:00
Pack up — life jackets away, get changed. Hand out sailing certificates!

Friday — Hobie Day

Graduation day. Big boat, big smiles. Every child goes home with a certificate.

9:00–9:30
Inside games
9:30–9:45
Finish remaining booklet pages
9:45–10:00
Snack break
10:00–10:15
Prep — bathroom, life jackets
10:15–11:40
Hobie Getaway rides — all kids on Hobie or split into two groups depending on class size. Kids not on Hobie get coach boat rides. Never stray too far from Hobie — it often struggles turning. Keep coach boat close.
11:40–12:00
Pack up — life jackets away, get changed. Hand out Beach Kids graduation certificates + stickers! 🎉
📚 Classroom Lessons — What to Teach ▼

⛵ Sailing Lesson

Used Tuesday and Thursday. Keep it fun — they don't need to know everything.

  • Show diagram of the boat. Name: Sail, Mast, Boom, Daggerboard, Sheet
  • Skip tiller and rudder on Tuesday — they're not steering today
  • Get kids to recall the parts once — don't drill it, they're 7
  • Get kids to draw the boat — see who includes the most parts. It's a competition.
  • Sail trim: "A flappy sail is NOT a happy sail. When in doubt, let it out."
  • One instructor pretends to be the wind — students use their arms to show where the sails should be
💡
Tacking dance: Teach the steps as a physical dance on Thursday — Look, Talk, Push tiller, Back foot across, Switch sides, Hand switch. Get them moving.

🚣 Canoe Lesson

Used Monday. Demo with a real paddle in the classroom.

  • Basic diagram: how to figure out which end is the front (seat with legroom in front of it)
  • How many people per bench
  • Where to sit when being towed — as far back as possible
  • Paddle grip: one hand on top, other gripping the side with thumb up
  • Push the water backwards. Pull paddle fully out on the way forward.
  • Don't go too fast or you'll splash everyone
  • Pass paddles around to try in the classroom
  • Form groups of 3–4. Practice paddling motion standing up — one boat at a time.
  • Everyone gets at least 2 practice attempts before hitting the water

Scavenger Hunt — Two Versions

📋 Checklist version

Find and tick off: seashell, seagull, bunny, windsock, driftwood, canoe, sailboat, wetsuit, coach boat, lifejacket, seaweed, rocks

🔍 Clue version

5-clue chain in envelopes. Final prize: pirate hats — kids wear them for the rest of the day.

  • Clue 1 → sailboat (mast clue)
  • Clue 2 → seashell (colourful, on the shore)
  • Clue 3 → bunny (ears up, by the boats)
  • Clue 4 → lifejacket (helps you float)
  • Clue 5 → pirate hats in the classroom

Rope & Knots

  • Tie a figure-8 knot
  • Tie up the instructor to a chair (kids love this)
🛡️ Safety & Contingencies ▼
⚠️
Every day, every session: Kids supervised at all times. Life jackets on near water — zipped and buckled. Shoes worn around the compound. Sunscreen applied. Hydration checked.

Wind Conditions

ConditionAction
Slightly too windyIncrease instructor involvement on sailboats — kids don't steer. Stay behind the breakwater. Stay very close to shore.
Way too windyDo not go on the water. Run shore-based activities instead. Full backup activity list in the Beach Kids Miscellaneous Activities doc.

Life Jacket Rules

  • Life jackets on before leaving the classroom for any water activity
  • Must be zipped and buckled — check every child individually
  • Not off until back from the water and away from the shoreline
  • Remember wetsuit sizes from Day 1 — saves significant time every day after

On-Water Safety

  • Coach boat on the water before any class boats launch
  • Maximum 2 kids per boat with 1 adult — no exceptions
  • On Hobie Day: never stray far from the Hobie — coach boat stays close at all times
  • For capsizing practice (shallow water only): instructor in wetsuit standing in water, kids tied to instructor on long lines
  • Stay behind the breakwater in any elevated wind
⚡ Roles at a Glance ▼

👤 Lead Instructor

  • In classroom 15 min before class starts
  • Runs classroom lessons and games
  • Coordinates with other classes for equipment
  • Makes all contingency calls
  • Manages parent interactions on Day 1
  • Awards certificates Thursday and Friday
  • Leads instructor debrief after session

⛵ Support Instructor

  • Brings kids from gate to classroom on Day 1
  • Helps with wetsuits and life jackets daily
  • Gets coach boat ready for water sessions
  • Sails with 1–2 kids per boat on Opti/mini sail days
  • Runs beach activities while other half is on water
  • Helps pull boats up and pack down
  • Supervises kids at all times — never leaves them unattended
💡
You're assisting, not just supervising. When kids are doing beach activities, get involved — join the sandcastle, play the beach game, get in the water on swim day. Kids this age respond to adults who are genuinely engaged.
